Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · Last updated 21 September 2026

This policy describes how SupaPulse (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use the iOS, Android, or web client. SupaPulse is a developer tool for managing your Supabase projects.

We do not store API keys, personal access tokens, OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, or service role keys in our database. Secrets used to talk to Supabase stay on your device. The way to revoke the app is to delete that token in your own Supabase account.

1. Who we are

SupaPulse is an unofficial third-party client. It is not a product of Supabase Inc. and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Supabase. Bundle ID: app.supapulse.mobile.

2. What the app does

You sign in with Google or GitHub through supabase.com (Supabase platform OAuth), or you paste a Supabase personal access token. The app then lists your organizations and projects and lets you inspect usage, run SQL, and work with tables, Auth users, Storage, and Edge Functions according to the scopes you granted.

3. Information stored on your device

  • A Supabase management credential (OAuth access token or personal access token sbp_…), in the OS keychain / keystore on native apps.
  • Optional refresh token and expiry time for OAuth sessions.
  • Optional profile name, email, and avatar returned by sign-in.
  • SQL snippets you save, alert preferences, the last selected project, and a short-lived project list cache.
  • A random install identifier used only to count unique app opens. It is not a secret.

On iOS this uses the system Keychain. We never put a management token in a remote database table. The app rejects service_role keys and JWT service keys if you try to paste them.

4. Information that may be stored on our servers

We operate a small backend (OAuth callback, Management API proxy, and an optional user-count function) on a Supabase project that belongs to the app, not to you.

  • User count record, with no secrets. We may save an identity label, email if Supabase returns one, auth method (Google / GitHub / token), organization name, and first/last seen timestamps. The upsert key is never the raw access token.
  • OAuth client secret. That secret belongs to our OAuth app on supabase.com. It is stored as an Edge Function secret so the app binary never contains it. It is not your project key.
  • Tokens in transit only. The OAuth callback exchanges an authorization code for tokens and returns them to the app. The Management proxy forwards your token to api.supabase.com for that request. We do not persist those tokens in our database.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not run third-party analytics trackers in the app for ads.

5. Information we do not collect

  • Your project service_role or anon keys.
  • The contents of your database beyond what you yourself query while using the app. Those results stay in the session on the device.
  • Payment card data. If the app is sold as a paid download, billing is handled by Apple.
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

6. Third parties

Sign-in and project APIs are provided by Supabase and, if you choose Google or GitHub, by those identity providers on supabase.com. Their privacy policies apply to that part of the flow. We do not receive your Google or GitHub password.

7. How to delete your data and revoke access

Because we do not keep your keys, deleting the credential in your Supabase account is the complete way to stop the app from accessing your projects.

  1. Go to https://supabase.com/dashboard/account/tokens.
  2. Delete the personal access token you used with SupaPulse. Access through that key stops immediately.
  3. If you used Google or GitHub, revoke the SupaPulse OAuth app from your Supabase organization settings.
  4. In SupaPulse, open Settings → Sign out. That wipes the token and local snippets from the device.
  5. Uninstall the app if you want it removed from the device.

To ask us to delete the optional user-count row (email or display label only), open an issue at github.com/MishaTokyo/supapulse-legal/issues.

8. Children

The app is a developer tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. The date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
