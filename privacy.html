Privacy Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

The short version

  • SupaPulse is a developer tool for watching your Supabase projects. It does not track you, does not show ads, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with data brokers.

Stored only on your device

  • Your Supabase session or personal access token, kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Saved SQL snippets, alert rules, the last project you opened, and a cached project list.
  • A personal access token is never stored on our server. When the app opens it is sent over HTTPS to look up your Supabase user ID and email, then discarded.

Stored on the SupaPulse server

  • Account: your Supabase user ID, email, display name, first organization ID and name, how you signed in, and when you first and last opened the app. We use this to run your account and to delete it when you ask.
  • Background alerts (only if you connect with Supabase sign-in and keep alerts on): your Supabase session encrypted with AES-256-GCM, up to five project IDs and names, your alert rules including their SQL, quiet hours, repeat interval and time zone, when each rule last fired, the title and text of alerts we sent you (kept 30 days for the in-app history), and this device’s push token.
  • The server reads project metrics through the Supabase Management API only to evaluate your alert rules. Read-only SQL from your rules runs against your project; results are not stored, only the number or fingerprint needed to tell if a rule fired.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the SupaPulse server and provides the Management API your data comes from.
  • Expo and Apple Push Notification service deliver alerts. They receive your push token and the alert text.
  • Signing in happens on supabase.com in a private browser window. Supabase’s own website may show its own cookie notice; SupaPulse does not read or keep those cookies, and the private window discards them when it closes.

Your choices

  • Turn off Project alerts or sign out to delete your alert subscription, alert history, and push token from our server.
  • Settings → Delete account removes your SupaPulse account, alerts, alert history, and push tokens from our server and clears this device. Your Supabase projects are never touched.
  • You can revoke SupaPulse at any time in your Supabase organization settings under OAuth Apps.

Children

  • SupaPulse is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children.
