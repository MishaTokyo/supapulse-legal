SupaPulse is an unofficial third-party companion for your own Supabase organization. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Supabase Inc. These pages describe how the app works, what we store, and how you remove access.

How the app works

You sign in with Google or GitHub on supabase.com, which authorizes your organization, or you paste a personal access token (sbp_…) from Account → Access Tokens. The app then lists your projects and lets you inspect usage, run SQL, and work with tables, Auth users, Storage, and Edge Functions on the project you select.

API calls run against your projects with your credential. We never ask for a service_role key. The app rejects those if you try to paste them.

Where data lives

We do not store API keys, personal access tokens, OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, or service role keys in our database. Those secrets stay on the device (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Our backend may see a token only in memory while completing sign-in or proxying a request. It is not written to tables or retained in logs.

On the device we keep the management token, optional refresh token, saved SQL snippets, alert preferences, and a short project list cache. Sign out deletes this from the device.

On our backend we may store a user-count record: an email or display label if Supabase returns one, the sign-in method, organization name, and timestamps. Never a token. The credential itself lives in your Supabase account. Delete it there and the app immediately loses access to your projects.

How to delete access

Because we do not keep your keys, the way to cut access is to revoke the credential in your own Supabase dashboard.

  1. Open supabase.com → Account → Access Tokens.
  2. Delete the personal access token you created for SupaPulse, or any token you pasted into the app. That is enough for that key.
  3. If you signed in with Google or GitHub, open your organization on supabase.com, go to OAuth Apps, and revoke SupaPulse.
  4. In the app, open Settings → Sign out. That deletes the token, snippets, and identity from this device.
  5. Uninstall the app if you want it removed from the phone.

There is no key vault on our side to erase. If you also want the optional user-count row removed, open an issue on this repository with the email you use on supabase.com.
