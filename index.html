SupaPulse is an unofficial third-party companion for your own Supabase organization. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Supabase Inc. These pages explain how the app works, what we store, and how you remove access.

How the app works

1. Sign in

Google, GitHub, or a token

Continue with Google or GitHub opens supabase.com so you can authorize your org. You can also paste a personal access token (sbp_…) from Account → Access Tokens.

2. Your projects

Pulse of the workspace

The app lists organizations and projects, then shows usage, SQL, tables, Auth users, Storage, and Edge Functions on the project you pick.

3. Your API

Talks to your Supabase

Queries and Management API calls run against your projects with your credential. We never ask for a service_role key.

We do not keep your keys in our database. Personal access tokens and OAuth access tokens stay on the device (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Our backend may see a token only in memory while completing sign-in or proxying a request. It is not written to tables, logs we retain, or backups of user data.

What lives where

On your phone

Secrets stay local

Management token, optional refresh token, saved SQL snippets, alert prefs, and a cached project list. Sign out wipes this device.

On our backend

No keys, ever

We may store a user record with email or a display label, auth method, and org name so we can count people who open the app. Never a PAT, refresh token, or service role key.

On your Supabase

You own the credential

The token or OAuth grant lives in your Supabase account. Delete it there and the app immediately loses access to your projects.

How to delete access

Apple asks us to explain account and data deletion. Because keys are not stored in our database, the reliable way to cut access is to revoke the credential in your own Supabase dashboard.

  1. Open supabase.com → Account → Access Tokens.
  2. Find the personal access token you created for SupaPulse (or any token you pasted into the app) and delete it. That is enough. The app can no longer call your projects with that key.
  3. If you signed in with Google or GitHub, also open your organization on supabase.com, go to OAuth Apps / authorized applications, and revoke SupaPulse.
  4. In the app, open Settings → Sign out. That deletes the token, snippets, and identity from this device.
  5. Uninstall SupaPulse if you want the app gone from the phone.

There is nothing extra you need us to “erase from our key vault,” because we do not have one. If you also want the optional user-count row (email / display label) removed from our app_users table, open an issue on this legal repo with the email you used on supabase.com.
